Hi! I am a Ph.D. candidate in Agricultural & Applied Economics at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.

I am an applied microeconomist working in behavioral and development economics, with a focus on decision-making, risk management, and agricultural investment. My job market paper combines structural modeling and lab-in-the-field experimentation to study how risk preferences shape household demand for financial risk management tools and the relationship between contract adoption and investment. Other areas of my work examine insurance contract design and government demand for disaster risk financing instruments.
